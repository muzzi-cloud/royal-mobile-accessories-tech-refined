ROYAL MOBILE ACCESSORIES — ADVANCED FRONT-END DEMO
==================================================

OPENING THE WEBSITE
1. Extract royal_mobile_accessories_advanced.zip.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Internet access is recommended for the remote stock photos and Google Fonts.
   The core layout and product catalogue are local files.

INCLUDED
- index.html: premium-style homepage and featured products
- shop.html: 25 product categories, live search, category/price filters and sorting
- cart.html: add/remove products, quantities and subtotal
- checkout.html: illustrative checkout form flow
- about.html: brand story concept
- contact.html: contact form preview
- style.css: responsive styling and mobile navigation
- products.js: all 25 product records and sample PKR prices
- app.js: catalogue rendering, cart and demo form interactions

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This is a front-end portfolio/demo website, not a production e-commerce system.
- Product names, prices and descriptions are examples, not verified inventory.
- Product images are illustrative stock photos and may not depict the exact item.
- The cart uses browser localStorage on the visitor's device.
- Checkout does not create a real order or take payment.
- Contact and newsletter forms do not send or store data.
- No admin panel, database, delivery integration, authentication or payment gateway is included.

BEFORE USING FOR A REAL BUSINESS
Replace sample products, product images, pricing, business details and placeholder email.
Add a secure backend/database, order processing, real contact form integration, shipping
rules, privacy/return policies and a supported payment provider. Test all content and links.
Do not present demo checkout, placeholder contact details or sample stock as real.
